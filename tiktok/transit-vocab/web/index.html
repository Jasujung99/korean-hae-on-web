Building, or end of the line

터미널

teo-mi-neol

A large transit building.
Ticket booths, waiting lounges, shops, and platforms.

종점

jong-jeom

The end of the line.
Where a bus or subway route finishes.

우리말샘에서는 같이 쓰일 수 있어요.

The dictionary allows both. On the street, Koreans keep them apart.

Quick check

지하철 1호선

O 종점 the last station
X 터미널 not for a subway line

The final station on a subway line is a 종점.

Quick check

마을버스

O 종점 even a roadside sign
X 터미널 no large building

If the route ends there, it is still a 종점.
Without a big building, it is not a 터미널.

Quick check

인천공항

O 터미널 a building for planes
X 종점 not the end of a route

You board or change planes in a 터미널.
The route does not end there, so it is not a 종점.

Does it stop, or pass through?

정차 停車

jeong-cha

The vehicle comes to a stop.
Briefly, on the road or at a station.

경유 經由

gyeong-yu

Going via a place.
An intermediate point on the way.

정차

버스가 완전히 정차한 뒤에
자리에서 일어나 주세요.

Please stand up only after the bus
has come to a complete stop.

이 급행열차는 이번 역에
정차하지 않고 통과합니다.

This express passes the station without stopping.

The place, and how you count

정류장 停留場

jeong-ryu-jang

A small roadside stop.
Usually a sign or a bench. Bus or taxi.

정거장 停車場

jeong-geo-jang

A station, or any stop.
Also how people count the stops left.

정류장

버스 정류장 143

집 앞 버스 정류장에서
143번 버스를 타면 돼요.

You can catch bus 143 at the stop
in front of the house.

A taxi stand is usually 택시 승강장.

정거장

1 2 3

여기서 시청역까지는
세 정거장만 더 가면 돼요.

From here to City Hall Station,
three more stops by subway.

버스 정거장 is also said. In everyday talk, 정거장 counts the stops.
